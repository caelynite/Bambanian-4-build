Overview

Holiday Decorations

The holidays at Disneyland are one of the most magical times of the year, transforming the park into a festive winter wonderland filled with lights, decorations, music, and holiday cheer. Usually beginning in early November and continuing into early January, the celebration features giant Christmas trees, colorful wreaths, and beautiful holiday displays throughout the park. Sleeping Beauty Castle shines with sparkling winter lights, while Main Street, U.S.A., is decorated with garlands and festive ornaments. Guests can enjoy the joyful atmosphere, take pictures with holiday decorations, and meet Disney characters dressed in their special holiday outfits.

Holiday Attractions

One of the highlights of the Disneyland holiday season is the variety of special attractions, entertainment, and seasonal food. Popular attractions receive holiday-themed transformations, including “it’s a small world” Holiday and Haunted Mansion Holiday, which combines Halloween and Christmas themes inspired by The Nightmare Before Christmas. Guests can also experience seasonal parades, festive music, and nighttime entertainment featuring dazzling lights and holiday magic. Throughout the resort, visitors can enjoy seasonal treats such as gingerbread cookies, peppermint desserts, holiday churros, and other special foods and drinks that make the celebration even more memorable.

Holiday Experiences

Overall, the holidays at Disneyland offer a unique experience for families, friends, and Disney fans of all ages. The combination of festive decorations, themed attractions, delicious seasonal treats, and beloved Disney characters creates an atmosphere filled with excitement and nostalgia. Whether visitors are enjoying the holiday entertainment, exploring the beautifully decorated park, or simply spending time with loved ones, Disneyland provides a special way to celebrate the season. Because the holiday period is popular, guests should plan ahead, check attraction schedules, and make park reservations when required to help ensure a fun and memorable holiday visit.

Holiday Menu

The holiday menu at Disneyland is filled with festive foods, sweet treats, and seasonal drinks that make the parks feel even more magical during Christmas. From peppermint-flavored desserts to gingerbread-inspired goodies, there is something for everyone to enjoy. Disney California Adventure also hosts the Festival of Holidays, featuring food inspired by different cultures and holiday traditions. Many of these items are available for a limited time, making them special treats for guests to try during their visit.

  • Mickey Elf Shake : A festive mint-and-chocolate cookie shake that is a fun, sweet holiday drink.
  • Milk and Cookies Tree-Shaped Macaron : A Christmas tree-shaped macaron decorated with festive details, perfect for a holiday photo and a sweet snack.
  • Gingerbread-Cranberry Cheesecake Trifle : A layered holiday dessert combining gingerbread flavors, creamy cheesecake, and tart cranberry.
  • Celebration Dinner Potato : A hearty baked potato loaded with savory toppings, making it a great option for guests craving something filling.
  • Braised Pork Belly Adobo : A savory Festival of Holidays favorite served with garlic fried rice, offering a flavorful alternative to holiday sweets.

Holiday Events

During the holiday season, Disneyland Resort offers a variety of festive events and entertainment that bring the magic of Christmas to life. Guests can enjoy the “A Christmas Fantasy” Parade, featuring Disney characters, dancing toy soldiers, colorful floats, and Santa Claus. At night, Sleeping Beauty Castle sparkles with holiday lights, creating a beautiful winter atmosphere throughout the park. Visitors can also experience seasonal entertainment, holiday music, character meet-and-greets, and special nighttime displays that make Disneyland feel extra magical. These events are popular with families and Disney fans who want to celebrate the holidays surrounded by their favorite characters and traditions. At Disney California Adventure, the holiday celebrations include Disney Festival of Holidays, which features live music, cultural dance performances, and foods inspired by different holiday traditions from around the world. Guests can watch World of Color – Season of Light, a nighttime spectacular that combines colorful fountains, special effects, and music from beloved Disney movies. Other festive experiences include Disney ¡Viva Navidad\!, which celebrates Latin American holiday traditions, and the Mickey’s Happy Holidays cavalcade, featuring Disney characters dancing along to cheerful music. Together, these events create a fun and welcoming atmosphere where guests can enjoy different cultures and make lasting holiday memories. These celebrations are scheduled for November 13, 2026, through January 6, 2027. +1
